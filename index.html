Hi there, welcome!

I am PhD student at Dimsdale-Zucker Context and Memory lab, where we study human long-term memory and its underlying neural mechanism.

Before joining Dr. Halle Dimsdale-Zucker's lab at UCR, I worked with Dr. Do-Joon Yi for my MA in Psychology, and I received by BA in Psychology, BS in Systems biology at Yonsei University, South Korea.
